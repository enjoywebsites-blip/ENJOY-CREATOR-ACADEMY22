STUDENT GUIDE / ACCESS

From enrollment to learning.

A practical guide to the ECA student journey, including how to enter the portal, open course materials, troubleshoot access and reach support.

01 / BEFORE YOU START

A simple route from purchase to practice.

The public site explains the programme. After enrollment, the student journey moves to the dedicated login and learning portal pages.

Because the current course resource is hosted externally, Google Drive permissions determine the final material access.

02 / STUDENT FLOW

Four steps.
Nothing hidden.

The student path is deliberately simple so the website does not become another layer to learn.

01

Complete enrollment

Use the ECA enrollment panel and proceed to the supplied Razorpay payment destination when you are ready.

02

Enter Student Login

Use the dedicated student entry page to start the portal flow from the website.

03

Open course materials

The learning portal takes you to the current Google Drive course resource supplied by ECA.

04

Keep the workflow moving

Work through the lessons in sequence, use the included community support, and return when new course material is added.

03 / WHEN ACCESS DOESN'T OPEN

A quick access check.

ACCOUNT

Use the Google account that has permission.

If Google Drive reports that you do not have access, check the active Google account before contacting ECA.

LINK

Open the current portal link.

Start from Student Login or Learning Portal so you use the current ECA entry route.

SUPPORT

Contact ECA when you are still blocked.

WhatsApp is the supplied direct channel. You can also email ECA with your purchase details.

04 / DEVICE EXPERIENCE

Desktop or mobile.
Keep learning.

The ECA website is designed to adapt to smaller screens. The course material itself follows the permissions and presentation of the external resource destination.

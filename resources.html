CREATOR RESOURCES

Think in workflows.
Not tool lists.

A practical reference page for creators exploring AI. Use it to understand how ideas, prompts, visuals, motion, voice and editing fit together before choosing individual tools.

01 / THE WORKFLOW

Idea → Prompt → Visual → Output.

Tools change quickly. A useful creator workflow stays understandable even when the individual tools evolve.

01

Start with intent

Define what you are making, who it is for and what the final output needs to communicate.

02

Write a useful prompt

Give the model enough direction to express subject, context, style, action and output requirements.

03

Build the visual

Generate or design a visual foundation before asking motion or video tools to do more work.

04

Add motion and sound

Combine movement, narration, music and transitions to turn a visual into a communication piece.

05

Edit for the audience

Remove noise, improve pacing and make the final piece easy to watch and understand.

02 / LEARNING DIAGNOSTIC

Before choosing a tool, ask these questions.

What am I trying to create? What must stay consistent? Which part needs human judgement? Which part can AI accelerate? What is the simplest tool that can get the job done?

The answers help you choose technology from the workflow outward, rather than collecting tools without a production system.

Does ECA teach only one AI tool?

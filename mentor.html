THE APPROACH

Build first.
Test constantly.
Teach what makes sense.

The academy's teaching philosophy comes from learning by doing: understand enough to start, build with the tools available, test the result, and improve the workflow.

The goal is practical understanding rather than presenting AI as a collection of isolated features.
